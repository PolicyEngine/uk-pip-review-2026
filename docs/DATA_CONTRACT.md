# Data contract

- **Engine and data pair.** Microcosm UK 2024-25 national build `uk-frs-calibration-attempt-20261112T133744Z-6f918b81` (SHA-256 `e268e3af7932784ad4471f008097a5acc6c3c5bc15c4c54fbb5a489b2a0ac9ae`, Hugging Face dataset `policyengine/populace-uk-private`) with policyengine-uk 2.177.7 and policyengine-core 3.34.10, the versions it was built and certified on (`data_build.DATA_BUILD`). No policyengine.py release covers the pair, so simulations load policyengine-uk directly; model mode refuses another engine version or file (`pipeline.CERTIFIED_BUNDLE`). Every figure carries "build uk-frs-calibration-attempt-20261112T133744Z-6f918b81, policyengine-uk 2.177.7, not in a policyengine.py release"; PIP-specific validation is pending (#1, #4).
- **PIP awards on 6f918b81.** The build stores each person's daily living and mobility assessment points for 2024 and no award category; policyengine-uk derives each year's award from the points against that year's thresholds. On build 6f918b81 the points are drawn only for people with a PIP award; everyone else holds -1 (not assessed). A recipient of one component has the other drawn below 8: daily living mostly over 4 to 7, mobility at 0 or 4 (the file's 359 mobility-only recipient rows hold daily living points of 4 to 7 in 295 cases and 0 to 3 in 64; 152 are at 6 or 7). So a cut to a standard threshold is not computable, alone or bundled with any other change: people turned down at those points are not in the data, and only existing recipients of the other component could move (a daily living threshold of 6 would move the mobility-only recipients drawn at 6 or 7, about 77k people from 88 distinct survey households; a mobility threshold of 5 to 7 moves no one, and 4 or lower moves only daily-living-only recipients drawn at 4). The model validator and the publication gate refuse any computable measure that holds such a cut (`data_limits.refusing_limits`). A threshold raise, and an enhanced threshold lowered but kept at 8 or more (12 to 10, say, which moves only standard-rate recipients of the same component, all in the data), are computable on the stated assumption that the drawn points place recipients correctly within their award; an enhanced threshold below 8 counts as a standard cut (`data_limits.LIMITS_BY_BUILD`; facts from María, data owner, 13 November 2026). It also stores the UC limited-capability flags (`uc_limited_capability_for_WRA`, `uc_limited_capability_for_work`), which the UC health gateway overrides from the stored values, and `cares_for_severely_disabled_person`, which gates the caring-hours route to Carer's Allowance and the UC carer element. It does not store `is_severely_disabled_for_benefits`, so policyengine-uk computes it from the PIP daily living amount (and DLA care, Attendance Allowance and AFIP), and it follows a change to the enhanced daily living award; `is_disabled_for_benefits` and `is_enhanced_disabled_for_benefits` are stored and do not. (Column names from the file's HDFStore metadata, 13 November 2026.)
- **Microdata stay private.** No `.h5` file, record-level extract or weight is committed (`.gitignore`, `scripts/check_public_data.py`).
- **Columns the PIP measures read:** `pip_dl_category`, `pip_m_category`, `dla_sc_category`, `dla_m_category`, `age`, `region`, `carers_allowance_reported`, `is_disabled_for_benefits`, `is_severely_disabled_for_benefits` (a formula on 6f918b81), `pip_dl_points`, `pip_m_points`, `uc_limited_capability_for_WRA` (stored; the gateway reads the stored values), `cares_for_severely_disabled_person`. `pip_dl`, `pip_m`, `pip_dl_category`, `pip_m_category`, `carers_allowance_pre_overlap`, `is_carer_for_benefits` and `uc_LCWRA_element` are formulas, not stored, so a modifier may wrap them.
- **England and Wales mask.** Every table keeps England and Wales households only (`pip_review.aggregate`). In a static run households do not interact, so this gives the figures a country test would.
- **Columns the PIP measures need but the data lack:** a score per activity, a health-condition group, hospital stays, award duration and review dates, and whom each carer cares for: the build stores whether a carer cares for someone with a qualifying award (`cares_for_severely_disabled_person`), not who or where that person is, so a carer of someone outside the household is not reached (`data_limits.MISSING_INPUTS`).
- **Heavy runs.** One data-loading job or production build at a time, under the machine-wide lock with at least 22 GB free+inactive (`pip_review.heavy`: the file `/tmp/pe-heavy.lock` holding "<pid> <label>", created with noclobber and removed only by its owner). Model mode runs one simulation per process; its record-level arrays stay in a temporary directory and are never published. The national file holds about 78k household rows from about 16k survey households, and its memory use cannot be cut much by trimming the file; the practical fix is one year per run (María, data owner, 13 November 2026). Model mode has no per-year option yet.
